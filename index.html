01 / About

A bit about my path

I’m a freshman at Ohio State studying computer science & engineering, with a business minor so I can think about technical work in the context of the people, systems, and decisions it affects. My interest in software began with research: at Cleveland Clinic I worked with biomedical data, and at Case Western I explored the biological mechanisms behind cancer treatment response. Those experiences taught me that the best technical work is grounded in real-world problems, clear communication, and thoughtful design.

I’m especially interested in the intersection of machine learning, health, and data-driven decision making, and I’m excited to keep building tools that make complex information more understandable and useful.
